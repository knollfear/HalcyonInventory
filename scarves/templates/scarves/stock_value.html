Retail is the asking price, not the price. Some of it goes at a discount, at the end of a season or years later, and a write-down is an ordinary thing. It is printed because it is the only honest upper bound — cost alone understates a shelf whose value add is the dyeing, none of which is priced in the cost column.
Nothing plans against this page. The bath column is a size a person can hold rather than a work order: dyeing is planned off par and off the Sunday close, and a bath count derived from what is on the shelf would be capacity asking for production.

A bath that was taken back is not on here. An entry undone on Produced Since leaves the original row exactly as written and adds a compensating one, so the question is whether an entry still stands rather than whether one was ever made. Those baths are named on the session and out of every figure.
An estimate is offered, never recorded. A session that closed before the figures were kept is valued at today's prices so a page of real work does not read as though it earned nothing — but the row itself still carries no money, and the estimate is marked wherever it is printed. Everything unmarked is what that session was actually worth on the day.
A session is dated by when its baths were checked in. Nothing records when a pot was lit, so a session dyed on Saturday and reported on Monday falls on the Monday, and a sheet worked across a boundary reports on each side the baths that were accepted there. What sold is on Square’s own clock instead, which is the one that does not move when an export is loaded late.
Added is not profit and not yet a wage. It is the asking price of what came out less what the blanks cost — the dye, the labour and the stall are all still inside it, and some of the retail will go at a discount. A rate for the dyeing would be paid out of this column; there is no rate here because none has been agreed.

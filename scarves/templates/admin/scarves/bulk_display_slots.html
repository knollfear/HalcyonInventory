How many of each colorway hang on the display when it is full — pegs times what a peg holds, or spots on the pole. This is capacity, not a target. Nothing here schedules production to fill it, and par is not touched: a bigger display is somewhere to put stock, never a reason to make more of it.

What it changes is the Sunday close. number_on_hand counts the display and the bag behind it together, so this number is where the app thinks the bag ends — and a product is asked about when its count drops to it. Zero means this never goes on display, so the close leaves it alone. Inactive finished products and stock on hand are untouched.

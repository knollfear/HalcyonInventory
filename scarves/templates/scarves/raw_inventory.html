Nothing here proposes a par. The columns are what there is to judge by. On pace projects units from the share of a season the weekends already banked took in earlier years — so it is only as good as the seasons behind it, and the count of those is printed with it. Entered is production somebody typed up, not production that happened: a quiet week there is a week nobody entered, and never a reason to order less.

{% else %}

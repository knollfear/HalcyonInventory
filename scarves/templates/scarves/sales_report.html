What this counts

Sale entries on the inventory log, and nothing else. That is narrower than "stock that left the tent": the Sunday close writes an adjustment when the count disagrees, and some of those really are sales nobody registered — but the app cannot tell which, and a guess in the same column as a till receipt is worse than a gap. Close History has that half.

The date is when the entry was written. A webhook lands within seconds of the sale, so those agree; a CSV loaded with import_square_sales piles the whole file onto the day it was imported; a resolved unidentified sale is back-dated to Square's own sale time. Only the middle one moves a total, and the table above says when it applies.

Value is units × today's price, not what the till took. A price that has changed since, or anything sold at a discount, makes it wrong — it is here to rank colorways against each other, not to reconcile against Square.

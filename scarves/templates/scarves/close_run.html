The app thinks the bag is empty for each of these, so you should be holding a tag. Fill the display first, then say how many you have altogether — display and bag together. Holding the tag means the bag's empty, so the answer is one of the buttons. No tag means there was stock in it after all: fill the display, count what's left, and put the total in the box.

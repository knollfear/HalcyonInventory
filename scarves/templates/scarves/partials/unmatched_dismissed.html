No undo here on purpose: dismissal is a timestamp and a reason, nothing is destroyed, and the admin clears it. This differs from the Sunday close's undo button because of who is holding the phone — that page has no login and its users have no accounts, so a fix they cannot make is a fix that goes unmentioned.

The two columns point at opposite ends of the pipeline, which is why they are never added together. Overcounts — the count came in below what the app held — are things that sold or left without registering: a swapped sale, a hand-keyed line at the till, or a webhook that has quietly stopped delivering.

That last one is worth the page on its own. A dropped sale physically becomes a tag in somebody's hand about a week later, so a dead webhook shows up here without any cross-check against Square at all.

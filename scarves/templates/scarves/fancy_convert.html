A scarf that gets extra line work stops being one product and becomes another. One colorway goes down, its fancy counterpart goes up.

You don't have to record it here. An unrecorded conversion still gets found — the plain one shows up as an overcount at its peg and the fancy one as an undercount at its, weeks apart. Doing it here is cheaper than the correction, and it's the only thing that can answer how many were fancied this season.

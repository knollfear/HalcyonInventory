Every other page in the app consumes these rows — the label sheet, the raw shelf forecast, the planner — and none of them ever showed the rows themselves, so recording a dye bath was a commitment with no receipt and no way out. The safe move was not to record it. **A retracted entry leaves no mark here.** Both rows drop out of the list — see `producedsince.entries` for the argument. A struck-through row reading "taken back" is a standing note about a mistake on a page somebody opens every week, and a correction that leaves one behind is a correction with a price on it. Nothing is hidden from the record: both rows stay in `InventoryLog`, and the admin shows them like it shows everything else. No confirmation dialog in front of Take back, deliberately.
